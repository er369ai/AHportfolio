Clients

What clients say.

Expertise

Six disciplines under one roof.

We cover the full spectrum of product engineering, so nothing gets lost in handoffs between separate vendors.

Backend architecture

Relational data models, clean migrations and versioned APIs built for long-term maintainability and zero schema drift.

UX/UI & interfaces

Mobile-first UX/UI designed against real operational data volumes and edge-case content, not static mockups.

Custom integrations

Third-party services isolated behind adapters with retries, timeouts and fallbacks, so external downtime never breaks your product.

Unattended automation

Background queues, idempotent job handlers, dead-letter processing and audit logs that give operators real control.

AI integration

Retrieval pipelines, prompt structure, structured output validation and token-cost control — and client-owned models where off-the-shelf isn’t enough.

Product & technical leadership

Direct work with your lead from scope definition through launch and the long operational tail afterwards.
