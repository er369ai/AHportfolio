FAQ

About A&H Techworld.

What does A&H Techworld do?

A&H Techworld is a technology company based in Nicosia, Cyprus. It is a three-person software product studio covering backend architecture, UX/UI design, custom integrations, unattended automation, AI integration and product leadership — from the first database schema through launch and ongoing operation.

Where is A&H Techworld based?

Nicosia, Cyprus. The registered office is Orfeos 2B, 1070 Nicosia, Cyprus. The company is registered in Cyprus as A&H TECH WORLD LTD, registration number HE 494576, and works with clients worldwide.

Is A&H Techworld the same as AH Techworld or AH Tech?

Yes. A&H Techworld, AH Techworld, A&H Tech World, AH Tech and A&H Techworld Ltd all refer to the same Cyprus company, A&H TECH WORLD LTD (HE 494576) in Nicosia, at ahtech.world.

Does A&H Techworld work with businesses across Cyprus?

Yes. The team is in Nicosia and works with companies anywhere on the island — Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos, Famagusta and North Cyprus, where it built and runs the KibRide ride-hailing platform — as well as clients abroad. Software development in Cyprus.

How big is the team at A&H Techworld?

Three people: one project lead and two developers. The same three work on every project end to end, so there is no handover between a sales team, a design team and a build team.

What has A&H Techworld built?

Four platforms are live in production: AutomateOnline, an automation platform for recurring online work; KibRide, a ride-hailing platform for North Cyprus; Mituteed, an Estonian public web platform; and Säästupark.

What services does A&H Techworld offer?

Backend architecture, UX/UI and interface design, custom third-party integrations, unattended automation, AI integration, and product and technical leadership.
